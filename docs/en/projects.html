Snake Remastered

Microsoft MakeCode Arcade

Fabruary 2026

Project created as part of the Discovery Stage Day organised by Ynov Campus Lyon
During the morning, we learnt the basics of programming logic by creating a game inspired by Snake on Microsoft MakeCode Arcade.
In my version, the player has three lives instead of one, he win the game once he reach 100 points, and for every 10 apples swallowed, a monster appears and chases the player. You must avoid it within the time limit to stay alive and continue the game.
